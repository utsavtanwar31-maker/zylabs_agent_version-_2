# Company Profile: **blinkit**

---

## Company Overview
**blinkit** (formerly known as Grofers) is an Indian quick‑commerce (q‑commerce) platform that promises ultra‑fast delivery of everyday essentials. The company operates a network of “dark stores” (dedicated fulfillment centers) across the country, enabling it to ship groceries, fresh produce, household supplies, and personal‑care items within minutes of an order being placed.  

- **Core proposition:** “delivering groceries, fresh produce, household supplies, and personal‑care products” 【1】  
- **Operational footprint:** Over **40 Indian cities** with **500+ dark‑store locations** 【3】
