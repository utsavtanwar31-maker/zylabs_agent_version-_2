# Company Profile: Zomato  

---

## Company Overview  
Zomato is an Indian‑based technology platform that connects consumers with restaurants. It markets itself as **“India’s #1 food delivery app”** and also provides B2B services to restaurants through its **Hyperpure** supply‑chain solution. The company’s core business revolves around facilitating online food ordering and delivery, while its ancillary offering supports restaurant operations with ingredient sourcing and logistics.
