# Company Profile: Databricks

## Company Overview
Databricks is a leading data and AI company that provides a unified platform for data engineering, data science, and machine learning. The company is renowned for its innovative solutions that help organizations leverage their data effectively to drive innovation and solve complex challenges. Databricks serves a global clientele, including over 70% of the Fortune 500 companies, and has more than 20,000 customers worldwide. [Source](https://www.databricks.com)

## What They Sell
Databricks offers a wide range of products and services designed to enhance data management, analytics, and AI capabilities:

1. **Lakebase**: A serverless Postgres database integrated with the lakehouse, built for the AI era. It merges the reliability of Postgres with the scale and analytics power of a data lakehouse. [Source](https://www.databricks.com/product/lakebase?itm_data=homepage-featuredproducts-tile1-lakebase)

2. **Genie**: A business intelligence tool that provides instant insights from enterprise data using natural language, eliminating the need for SQL or coding. [Source](https://www.databricks.com/product/genie?itm_data=homepage-featuredproducts-tile2-genie)

3. **Unity Gateway**: An AI governance solution that offers unified access, cost control, observability, and guardrails for enterprise AI. [Source](https://www.databricks.com/product/artificial-intelligence/unity-gateway?itm_data=homepage-featuredproducts-tile3-unitygateway)

4. **Lakehouse**: A serverless data warehousing solution that combines the storage of a data lake with the performance of a data warehouse. [Source](https://www.databricks.com/product/databricks-sql?itm_data=homepage-featuredproducts-tile4-lakehouse)

5. **Databricks Data + AI Platform**: A comprehensive platform that integrates data engineering, data science, and machine learning. [Source](https://www.databricks.com)

6. **Agent Bricks**: A platform for building and governing AI agents that improve decision-making using enterprise context. [Source](https://www.databricks.com/product/artificial-intelligence/agent-bricks)

7. **Databricks SQL**: A powerful SQL analytics tool for data analysts and business intelligence. [Source](https://www.databricks.com/product/databricks-sql)

8. **Databricks Machine Learning**: Tools and frameworks for building and deploying machine learning models. [Source](https://www.databricks.com/product/machine-learning)

9. **Databricks Delta**: A storage layer that enhances data lakes with reliability and performance. [Source](https://www.databricks.com/product/delta)

10. **Databricks Data Science & Engineering**: A collaborative environment for data scientists and engineers. [Source](https://www.databricks.com/product/data-science-engineering)
