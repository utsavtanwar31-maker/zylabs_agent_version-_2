# Company Profile: Zapier

## Company Overview
Zapier is a leading automation platform that enables businesses to connect their applications and automate workflows. With a focus on enhancing productivity and efficiency, Zapier provides tools that integrate with over 9,000 applications, catering to a diverse range of industries and company sizes globally. The platform is designed to support everyone from Fortune 500 companies to first-time entrepreneurs.

## What They Sell

### Zapier MCP
- **Category**: Automation Platform
- **Description**: Zapier MCP allows businesses to connect their AI tools to the apps they use, facilitating the creation of integrated workflows across more than 9,000 applications.
- **Features**: Works across leading AI tools, offers instant access to a vast array of apps, and provides granular permissions for team members.
- **Source**: [Zapier MCP](https://zapier.com/mcp)

### Next Gen Zaps
- **Category**: Workflow Automation
- **Description**: These are automated workflows that operate without manual intervention, featuring advanced logic and self-healing capabilities to ensure reliability and cost-effectiveness over time.
- **Features**: Workflows that run autonomously, self-healing capabilities, and improved cost efficiency.
- **Source**: [Next Gen Zaps](https://zapier.com/workflows)

## Case Studies

### Mercari US
- **Industry**: E-commerce
- **Geography**: United States
- **Products Used**: Zapier
- **Use Case**: Automating customer service ticket resolution
- **Outcome**: Resolved 47,000 tickets monthly, saving over 3,000 agent hours each month.
- **Source**: [Mercari US Case Study](https://zapier.com/customer-stories/mercari)

### Okta
- **Industry**: Technology
- **Geography**: Global
- **Products Used**: Zapier
- **Use Case**: Automating credential rotations
- **Outcome**: Reduced process time from a full-day, multi-person task to 2 hours, with 13% of escalations handled automatically.
- **Source**: [Okta Case Study](https://zapier.com/customer-stories/okta)

### Palo Alto Networks
- **Industry**: Cybersecurity
- **Geography**: Global
- **Products Used**: Zapier
- **Use Case**: Cost savings through automation
- **Outcome**: Achieved annual savings of $150,000, equivalent to the cost of one full-time employee, benefiting over 3,000 internal users.
- **Source**: [Palo Alto Networks Case Study](https://zapier.com/customer-stories/palo-alto-networks)
