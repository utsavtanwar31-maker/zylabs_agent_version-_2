# Company Profile: bachatt  

---

## Company Overview  
**Bachatt** is a fintech platform that delivers a range of financial products through a single mobile application. The company’s value proposition centers on providing convenient, on‑demand access to investment and credit solutions, all managed within its proprietary app.  

*Evidence:* The official website states that “Insta Fund, mutual funds, gold & silver, loans and credit cards — all from one app”【1】.

| Product / Service | Description | Source |
