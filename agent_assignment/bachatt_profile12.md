# Company Profile: Bachatt

## Company Overview
Bachatt is a comprehensive savings application designed to cater to the diverse financial needs of the Indian population. The platform provides a variety of financial products and services, aiming to simplify and enhance the savings experience for its users. Bachatt collaborates with SEBI- and RBI-regulated partners to ensure secure and reliable financial transactions.

## What They Sell
Bachatt offers a wide range of financial products, including:
- **Insta Fund**: A quick and accessible fund option for immediate financial needs.
- **Mutual Funds**: Investment opportunities in mutual funds for wealth growth.
- **Gold & Silver Investments**: Options to invest in precious metals like gold and silver.
- **Loans**: Various loan products to meet different financial requirements.
- **Credit Cards**: Credit solutions for everyday and business expenses.

Source: [Bachatt Official Website](https://bachatt.app)

## Who They Sell To
Bachatt targets a broad audience across India, including:
- **Salaried Professionals**: Individuals earning a regular income from employment.
- **Shop Owners**: Small business owners managing retail outlets.
- **Jewellers**: Professionals and businesses in the jewelry industry.
- **Dealers**: Individuals or companies involved in trading goods.
- **Manufacturers**: Businesses engaged in the production of goods.
- **Factory Owners**: Owners of manufacturing facilities.
- **Workers**: Employees working in various sectors mentioned above.

Source: [Bachatt Official Website](https://bachatt.app)
